Origins

About Kal Mydas.

Why Kal Mydas exists

Kal Mydas is not just a set of algorithms. It is a decentralised protocol built around gold and bitcoin, designed for the autonomy of those who take part in it. The algorithms are its first piece, not its last. The project was born from a simple question. Why does algorithmic trading, a common tool at banks, stay out of reach of the public?

Our approach

Our 5 gold robots (XAUUSD) are developed and optimised on MetaTrader 4. They were simulated on the broker’s real historical data, tested in live conditions, then stress-tested on the 2008 and 2020 crises. A sixth strategy, GENESIS, trades bitcoin.

The team

Kal Mydas is designed, coded and signed by its founder, based in Switzerland. He signs under the pseudonym Kal. He develops, deploys and monitors the protocol himself. A circle of contributors has been with him since the first months.

Risk warning

We promise nothing. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Your deposit carries its share of the gains and losses of the protocol reserve and can fall to zero. In simulation, some strategies went through temporary losses of up to 70%. We publish the source code and the test histories.

Frequently asked questions

Our answers.

What exactly is Kal Mydas?

Kal Mydas is a protocol on Base that offers algorithmic trading strategies, 5 on gold (XAUUSD) and GENESIS on bitcoin. The robots execute trades automatically. The status of each strategy can be read live in the app. Every trade can be verified on the Base chain.

What is the minimum deposit?

The minimum amount depends on the strategy. It is read from the contract and shown when you deposit, in USDC on Base. There is no maximum amount.

How do withdrawals work?

You withdraw with no Pass and no delay, at the current share price, within the USDC present in the pool. Withdrawals are suspended only during an emergency pause decided by the contract owner. No withdrawal fees. You deal directly with the contract.

Is future performance guaranteed?

No. The figures shown are historical simulations of the robots, run on the broker’s real data, over up to 21 years. They are not deposit results. Your deposit carries its share of the gains and losses of the protocol reserve and can fall to zero.

What are the fees?

No entry or exit fees. A performance fee of 20% on each strategy, 10% for a deposit in KAL, taken on new gains thanks to the High-Water Mark. Nothing is taken on a loss. No management fees. The access Pass is paid separately.

Do I need a crypto wallet to use Kal Mydas?

No. KALpay, the ecosystem’s wallet, is created on home.kalmydas.com, on your device, with no account and no email. You can also connect MetaMask or a wallet through WalletConnect.

Which strategy should I choose as a beginner?

HORIZON is the most cautious, designed for a first-step profile. VALKYRIE offers a balanced profile. The more aggressive strategies, REVOLUTION, TREASURY and ORION, require a clear understanding of volatility. You can take several and spread your capital as you wish.

Does KAL burn?

The protocol never burns KAL, by choice. KAL bought back with performance fees goes through the BuybackRecirculator contract and returns to the KalSwap KAL/USDC reserve. This rule can be checked on Basescan. It holds through the protocol’s practice, not through the token’s code.
